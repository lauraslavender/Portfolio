NIQ page launch

Two looping animations I made in After Effects to announce the launch of NIQ's Digital Commerce page.

Client
NIQ for Digital Commerce
Year
2025
Role
Motion designer
Tools
After Effects

The challenge

The launch needed a strong visual that would grab attention in a fast-scrolling feed, without being overwhelming. Both pieces had to loop seamlessly to look polished, and win over stakeholders inside NIQ as well as the audience outside it.

Results

Stakeholders liked how simple they were, and the loops helped the announcement get more visibility.
