Event design

The Happiest Hour

The visual identity for an NIQ × Skai cocktail reception at the Digital Shelf Summit 2025, inspired by New Orleans voodoo.

Client
NIQ × Skai
Year
2025
Role
Designer & illustrator
Scope
Invitations, menu, napkins

The brief

To celebrate the partnership between NIQ and Skai, I created the identity and event assets for The Happiest Hour: a cocktail reception where clients, partners and internal teams could connect in a relaxed, memorable setting.

The challenge was balancing a fun, voodoo-themed party with the more corporate tone of a B2B event. I paired NIQ's blues with playful illustrations and bold, editorial-style type.

01

The invitation

Invitations

Printed and digital invitations designed as tarot cards set the tone: moons, stars, an all-seeing eye and a hand holding the NIQ × Skai crystal ball over a city skyline. On the back, “We see big things in your future” leads into the evening's activities, from tarot readings to palmistry.

02

Menu & napkins

Menu

The menu mixes playful icons with brand-driven names. Each brand gets its own signature drink, the SkaiGarita and the NIQ Voodoo, with custom illustrations, alongside a list of house classics.

It was designed for print and screens.

Napkins

Custom illustrations and bold type. People took them home as keepsakes.

Outcome

Guests loved it, and it made a simple cocktail hour feel like a proper brand moment.
