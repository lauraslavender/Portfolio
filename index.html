About

My name is Laura! I'm a brand designer living and working in Paris.

For 6 years I've worked mostly with B2B SaaS and tech companies, across brand identity and systems, campaigns, web, presentations, illustration, print, events and motion. I enjoy building the systems that let an idea work consistently across every channel.

At NIQ for Digital Commerce, I helped shape and evolve Data Impact's identity through its international growth and acquisition. Most recently, I was the first in-house brand designer at B2B tech company Fabriq.

I've always worked inside marketing teams, and with a Master's in Digital Communication & Creation from ISCOM, I care as much about positioning, audience and messaging as about the craft. Originally from the US and based in Paris, I'm a native English speaker, fluent in French and advanced in Spanish, and I'm passionate about human-centred design, social and environmental justice, and good coffee.
