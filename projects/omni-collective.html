Branding case study

The Omni Collective: a podcast that pops

A fun, funky brand for the podcast from Data Impact by NielsenIQ. It complements the main brand but stands completely apart from it.

Client
Data Impact by NielsenIQ
Year
2023
Role
Brand designer
Tools
Figma

The brief

Data Impact wanted its podcast to have its own identity. A separate brand would make the posts feel less corporate and less like sponsored content, which encourages more organic interaction.

Competitor podcasts looked dry and very standard. The goal was something that would pop out from the rest.

01

Finding the logo

Iteration

The brief was open-ended, so I went through several rounds. Each early version was turned down for feeling too corporate. The long name made it hard to fit everything into one logotype.

The answer was a chunky, 1980s-inspired display font for “Omni”, framed by a minimal sans serif for the rest. The “OC” mark doubles as a playful repeating pattern.

02

Colour & type

Related, not identical

The palette is a simplified version of NielsenIQ's, with the darker main colours removed. It stays coherent with the parent brand while feeling clearly different.

Modak gives the logo its personality. For text, I first tested Aktiv Grotesk from the main brand, but it felt too corporate, so I switched to classic Helvetica, which sits better beside the logo.

03

In use

Covers & headers

A handful of templates gives lots of variety: episode covers mix the full palette with complementary or contrasting text. LinkedIn headers add punchy star shapes for extra playfulness.

Process

I designed and stored every asset in Figma, from the first logo sketches to the final visuals.

Takeaway

I love the end result. It took time and plenty of experimenting with the stakeholders to find what they wanted, but the final brand creates exactly the effect we were after.
