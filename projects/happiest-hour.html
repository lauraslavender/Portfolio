Event design

The Happiest Hour

A visual identity for an NIQ × Skai cocktail reception at the Digital Shelf Summit 2025, inspired by the mystique and charm of New Orleans voodoo.

Client
NIQ × Skai
Year
2025
Role
Designer & illustrator
Scope
Invitations, menu, napkins

The brief

To celebrate the partnership between NIQ and Skai, I created the identity and event assets for The Happiest Hour: a cocktail reception where clients, partners and internal teams could connect in a relaxed, memorable setting.

The visuals had to blend the playful energy of a themed party with the professionalism of a B2B networking event.

The challenge

Balancing the visual richness of a voodoo theme with the clean, corporate tone a business event needs. I paired NIQ's palette of blues with playful illustrations and bold, editorial-style type.

01

The invitation

Tarot cards

Printed and digital invitations designed as tarot cards set the tone: moons, stars, an all-seeing eye and a hand holding the NIQ × Skai crystal ball over a city skyline. On the back, “We see big things in your future” leads into the evening's activities, from tarot readings to palmistry.

02

Menu & napkins

Signature cocktails

The menu mixes playful icons with brand-driven names. Each brand gets its own signature drink, the SkaiGarita and the NIQ Voodoo, with custom illustrations, alongside a list of house classics.

It was designed for print and for screens, balancing themed fun with professional polish.

Keepsake napkins

Custom illustrations and bold type brought the theme to life in a tactile way. The napkins were practical, but also little keepsakes that carried the event's identity home.

Outcome

The suite sparked curiosity and got people involved while keeping both brands clear. Guests loved how memorable and delightful it was, and it turned a simple cocktail hour into a curated brand moment.
