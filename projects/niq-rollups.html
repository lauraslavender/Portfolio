NIQ rollups

Two rollup banners for NIQ for Digital Commerce events and client meetings in Paris and London.

Client
NIQ for Digital Commerce
Year
2025
Role
Designer
Tools
Adobe Illustrator

The brief

The team needed rollups for events in both Paris and London: brand-aligned pieces that would show off NIQ's digital commerce expertise in meetings with potential clients.

Rollup 1 covers NIQ's core digital commerce capabilities, key messages and positioning. Rollup 2 focuses on data-driven insights and the value for clients and partners.

01

Top to bottom

Reading flow

Each rollup leads the eye down the banner: a photo to catch attention, a bold headline, a few key points about the business with simple icons, and a call to action with a QR code and email at the bottom.

I built the print files in Adobe Illustrator and handed them over to the printer.

Results

The rollups gave the team a consistent look at events in both cities.
