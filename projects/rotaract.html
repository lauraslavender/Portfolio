Rotaract Paris Quai d'Orsay: a brand kit anyone can use

A social media branding kit and set of templates for my Rotaract club, so every member can create on-brand posts quickly, without the guesswork.

Client
Rotaract Paris Quai d'Orsay
Year
2022
Role
Designer (volunteer)
Tools
Figma

The brief

Create a social media branding kit with a series of templates, built on the existing Rotaract brand, that other club members could easily reuse.

The insight

Most members made our social posts in Canva, each in their own way. I wanted a shared set of guidelines and simple, repeatable templates that would take the guesswork out of it and bring the club's look together.

The Rotaract brand was already defined, so I worked within tight guidelines. I wanted the bright blue and pink to feel fun and playful, not overpowering. Subtle gradients, patterns and minimal icons give the colours room to speak.

01

Instagram stories

A story for every occasion

Templates for recruiting new members, meeting announcements, event recaps, cleanup days, new-post teasers and special evenings like our Movember night. Members just swap in their photos and text.

02

Carousels

Meet the team

A member carousel introduces the club's board and members, one slide each, with their role and the flags of their home countries, a nice nod to how international the club is. A progress bar at the top of each slide shows how far through the carousel you are.

03

Podcast

Inspired by Rotaract

The kit also covers the club's podcast, with a cover and episode cards for Spotify in the same pink, so it's instantly recognisable as part of the family.

Process

I built and stored everything in Figma because it's widely accessible and fairly easy for anyone to pick up.

Takeaway

I loved creating something practical for my club. The kit cut down the time we spent on communications and made the whole team's work easier and more efficient.
