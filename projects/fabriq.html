Fabriq Tour 2025: the teaser

A LinkedIn teaser for Fabriq's tour of five client factories across France. The goal: make manufacturing feel youthful, energetic and worth watching.

Client
Fabriq
Year
2025
Role
Motion designer & editor
Tools
After Effects

The brief

Fabriq's marketing team visited five client factories in five regions of France to document how manufacturing is evolving, each visit telling a different story.

Before the full series came out, they needed a short teaser for LinkedIn to build anticipation among clients, partners and prospects: capturing the energy of the journey without giving the story away.

01

The idea

A journey

Manufacturing isn't usually seen as the most exciting visual territory. I wanted to take something often thought of as dry and old-fashioned and make it feel youthful and alive.

The concept is travel and discovery: five days, five regions, five factories. Luggage stickers act as destination stamps for each region, and snippets of real tour footage keep it authentic.

A recurring illustrated train ties it together. It tells the story of a journey, and nods to Fabriq's eco-friendly travel: whenever possible, the team takes the train between regions and countries instead of flying.

02

The making

On brand, but more alive

Rather than inventing a new style, I extended Fabriq's existing illustrations, designing the train, suitcase and other assets as natural additions, then pushed the whole thing somewhere more lively and unexpected.

Each factory gets its own visual beat, reflecting the personality of each client, while the overall rhythm stays snappy and cohesive.

I picked clips from the raw tour footage, designed every motion asset from scratch and edited the piece in After Effects, from concept to delivery in about 30 hours.

Outcome

Timed with France's Industry Week, the teaser drew strong engagement from Fabriq's French audience and built anticipation for the full video series.
