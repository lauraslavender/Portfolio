5 applications of AI for the digital shelf

An ebook that turns complex AI topics into clear, practical insight for digital commerce professionals, and positions NIQ as a thought leader where AI meets ecommerce.

Client
NIQ for Digital Commerce
Year
2025
Role
Designer
Format
Interactive PDF ebook

The brief

NIQ for Digital Commerce wanted to show how brands can use AI to boost product visibility, sales and competitive edge on the digital shelf.

The ebook had to look polished and on brand, make complex AI applications accessible to a broad audience, and drive engagement and leads.

01

Design approach

Structure

A clean grid keeps the reading flow logical and easy on the eye. It became the standard layout for every NIQ ebook that followed. Bold headings, subheadings and callouts make it easy to scan for the key takeaways.

The look follows NIQ's brand guidelines for type and colour, balancing corporate credibility with a modern, digital feel.

Making data readable

Infographics and charts turn complex ideas into something digestible, like the timeline of AI's evolution and the nested diagram of AI, machine learning, deep learning and GenAI. Icons flag key points for quick reference.

In the digital version, call-to-action buttons point readers to further resources and NIQ's services, and hyperlinks make it easy to jump between sections and related reports.

Challenges

I worked with the content manager to distil the AI applications into clear, actionable insights. Rounds of design reviews kept the ebook innovative while protecting NIQ's authoritative voice.

Results

The ebook strengthened NIQ for Digital Commerce's position as a thought leader in AI for the digital shelf, increased engagement and lead capture, and earned positive feedback from industry professionals on its clarity and readability.
