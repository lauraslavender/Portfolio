Motion

NIQ for Digital Commerce: page launch

Two looping animations announcing the launch of NIQ's Digital Commerce page. Minimal, on brand, and made to stop the scroll.

Client
NIQ for Digital Commerce
Year
2025
Role
Motion designer
Tools
After Effects

The challenge

The launch needed a strong visual that would grab attention in a fast-scrolling feed, without being overwhelming. Both pieces had to loop seamlessly to look polished, and win over stakeholders inside NIQ as well as the audience outside it.

The approach

  • Minimal: clean animation that complements NIQ's branding without clutter.
  • Seamless loops: no visible restart, so the motion builds a steady rhythm.
  • Subtle but punchy: enough movement to catch the eye, never enough to distract.
  • On brand: NIQ's colours, typography and animation pacing throughout.

Results

Stakeholders loved the balance of simplicity and energy, and the loops gave the launch announcement extra visibility and interaction across NIQ's channels.
